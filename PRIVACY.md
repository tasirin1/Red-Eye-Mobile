# Privacy Policy — Red Eye / Parental Control

## What this app does
This app helps parents supervise their child's device. With the device owner's
permission it collects SMS messages, call history, contacts, location,
photos, and screen captures. Screen captures use either the Android
screen-capture consent you approve in Setup or, if you turn it on, an
accessibility service whose only job is to take screenshots on request.

## Where data goes
Collected data is sent **only** to the Telegram bot configured by the parent
in the app's Setup page. Data is never sold and never shared with third
parties.

> Note: notification forwarding includes the full text of incoming
> notifications (this can contain one-time codes, bank alerts, or private
> messages), and photos, video, screen captures, audio clips, and location are
> sent on demand via bot commands. Everything sent to the bot is stored by
> Telegram under the operator's account and its retention policy.

## Control
Monitoring can be stopped at any time by disabling it in the app or by
uninstalling the app.

## Contact
The app operator (the parent who configured the bot) is the data controller.
