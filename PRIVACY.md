# Privacy Policy — Red Eye / Parental Control

## What this app does
This app helps parents supervise their child's device. With the device owner's
permission it collects SMS messages, call history, contacts, location, and
photos.

## Where data goes
Collected data is sent **only** to the Telegram bot configured by the parent
(`BOT_TOKEN` / `CHAT_ID`). Data is never sold and never shared with third
parties.

> Note: notification forwarding includes the full text of incoming
> notifications (this can contain one-time codes, bank alerts, or private
> messages), and photos, audio clips, and location are sent on demand via bot
> commands. Everything sent to the bot is stored by Telegram under the
> operator's account and its retention policy.

## Control
Monitoring can be stopped at any time by disabling it in the app or by
uninstalling the app.

## Contact
The app operator (the parent who configured the bot) is the data controller.
